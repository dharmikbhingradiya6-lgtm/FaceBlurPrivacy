Project Title

Privacy Protection System Using Automatic Face Detection and Face Blurring


Project Objective

The objective is to automatically detect human faces in images, videos, or webcam streams and blur those faces to protect the identity of individuals.

This type of technology is widely used in:

Google Street View
YouTube videos
CCTV footage
News broadcasting
Dashcam videos
Social media content
Public surveillance systems


Problem Statement

Many images and videos shared online contain identifiable faces, creating privacy concerns. Manually hiding faces is time-consuming and impractical. The proposed system automatically detects and blurs faces, protecting personal privacy while preserving the rest of the visual content.


Scope

The system should work with:

Image files (.jpg, .png)
Videos (.mp4)
Webcam/live camera
Multiple faces
Unknown people

Software Required
Python 3.11+
OpenCV
NumPy
Matplotlib
MediaPipe (recommended)
Tkinter or Streamlit (optional GUI)
